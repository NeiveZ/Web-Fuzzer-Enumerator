# Ethics

WFEX is intended for authorized security testing, education, lab work and defensive assessments.

Allowed use:
- Your own systems
- CTF and lab targets
- Client environments with written permission
- Internal security validation

Disallowed use:
- Unauthorized scanning
- Credential attacks against third parties
- Disruption or persistence outside an approved scope
- Attempting to bypass access controls
- Collecting or exposing secrets without authorization
